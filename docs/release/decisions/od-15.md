## Question

How does the Combobox virtualise long option lists (REQ-CMP-72, CC-CMP-06)?

## Options

- **(a) Owned windowed list (default).** This is open PR #242
  (`next-fin/cmp-72-virtual`, head `2e6d302c6` at the time of this draft): "feat(combobox):
  owned windowed list on subscribeFrame, no tanstack".
  - Claims made by #242's description, which are not yet verified in CI because
    no pipeline exists (OD-8):
    - a `scrollTop` ref plus one `subscribeFrame` recompute derives the render
      window (overscan 5);
    - at most `visible + 2*overscan` rows are mounted, which the description gives
      as 19 rows at 500 items and ≤60 at 10,000;
    - `aria-setsize` is the total and `aria-posinset` the 1-based index on every
      row;
    - the list loads through `React.lazy` and stays out of the base chunk;
    - no `@tanstack/react-virtual` import.
  - If you choose (a): #242 merges in its PROMPT-FINAL v2 §3.3 N-E slot
    (`#238 → … → #241 → #242 → #243 → #337`), and #337 drops its virtualizer
    rewrite. #242 survives over #337's rewrite.
- **(b) Allowlist amendment.** Amend the `scripts/ci/verify-deps.mjs` allowlist
  so `src/components/combobox/**` may import `@tanstack/react-virtual`. This is
  contract item C-11 in `contract/v1.2-final` and is gated on OD-16. If you choose
  (b): #242 closes, C-11 is implemented, and FIN-C edits the allowlist.
