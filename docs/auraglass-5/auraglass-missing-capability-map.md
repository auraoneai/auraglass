# AuraGlass 5.0 — Missing-Capability Map (Deliverable F)

Sources: `docs/auraglass-5/component-inventory.json` (500 records = 496 component records + 4 meta/NOTE rows; queried with node, scores are `overall` on a 0–10 scale; dispositions over the 496: CONSOLIDATE 157, REMOVE 152, REDESIGN 75, POLISH 49, DEPRECATE 34, REPLACE 22, KEEP 7), `rg` over `src/` (stories and tests excluded), the autopsy reports in `docs/auraglass-5/autopsy/`, and `docs/auraglass-5/research/competitors.md`. Verdicts in the adversarial Verification sections were honored. Where a finding was PARTIAL, the corrected claim is used. Where two verification passes disagree, both verdicts are given.

Runtime evidence: `autopsy/runtime-remote.md` and `autopsy/remote-evidence/` (624 page loads in remote headless Chromium 141, software raster, at 1440×900 and 390×844; 42 stories over default, white, black and busy backgrounds, plus reduced-motion, `contrast: more` and `forced-colors` on 12 stories). Background adaptation, contrast, a11y media modes, FPS and backdrop-filter density are now measured in a browser. Limits: the input was the prebuilt `storybook-static/` (dated 2026-09-05 and not regenerated with source changes, storybook-showcase.md:86), not a fresh build of HEAD `src`; FPS is rAF cadence under software raster; contrast samples at most 5 text runs per capture. Per-component visual quality outside those 42 stories still rests on certification JSON and pixel statistics, and the screenshots were not viewed for this map. In practice, "exists" means the code exists. It does not mean the component looks premium.

Priority scale:
- P0: blocks a credible 5.0. A table-stakes surface is fake or missing.
- P1: expected by product teams in 2026.
- P2: differentiating, reusable surface.
- P3: niche work, or a labs-only surface.

Justification rule: each target is a surface that shadcn, Base UI, React Aria, MUI X or AI Elements already ships (competitors.md:22-26, :80, :92, :96, :121). None is a novelty. Exceptions are marked where competitors.md has no evidence (Kbd, spatial). The background-adaptive material row is also an exception: no mainstream library ships a refractive glass material (competitors.md:27), so its bar is platform material (Apple Liquid Glass, Windows Mica/Acrylic; runtime-remote.md §1), not a competitor component.

rg scope note: "rg X = 0" means a case-sensitive search over `src/` excluding `*.stories.*`, `*.test.*` and `__tests__`, re-run during review on 2026-10-06.
